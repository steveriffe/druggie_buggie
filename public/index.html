⚖️ The "Graduation" Arbitrage: Code 01 vs Code 07

UB-04 Claim Form
UB-04 CODE 01 "Graduated"
Routine Discharge / Planned Completion
  • Captures Completion Bonus: PPO case-rate reconciliations and quality milestone bonuses ($3k–$10k) paid in full.
  • Audits Suppressed: Claims marked routinely completed; low risk of insurer SIU retrospective audits.
  • Clawback Shield: Prior 28 days of $80k+ billing protected from retroactive necessity denials.
UB-04 CODE 07 "Left AMA"
Left Against Medical Advice
  • Bonus Forfeited: Insurer withholds completion and episode closure payments immediately.
  • SIU Audit Triggered: Insurer investigates why acute inpatient care was billed if patient walked out early.
  • Clawback Catastrophe: Insurer challenges medical necessity and demands refund of $30,000–$60,000+.
Investigative Takeaway: When a patient with a top-tier Aetna PPO says "Day 30 is my last day, even if I have to leave AMA," the facility cannot afford an AMA on the UB-04. By instantly declaring the patient "Graduated," the facility protects the \$87,500 billing run, pockets the completion bonus, and insulates itself against audit clawbacks.

✈️ The Interstate Fly-In Pipeline & The "Hostage Return Ticket"

How out-of-state Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS) plans are funneled into Dana Point, and how geographic distance is weaponized for forced retention.

⚠️ California Health & Safety Code § 11831.6
1. THE TARGET BCBS Oklahoma / HCSC
Out-of-State PPO Arbitrage

Targeting patients with robust out-of-network Blue Cross Blue Shield plans from Oklahoma, Texas, and Midwestern industrial employers. The BlueCard clearinghouse enables out-of-network charge master billing exceeding $60,000–$100,000 per episode.

2. THE BAIT Paid Inbound Flight
The Free Plane Ticket (Illegal Inducement)

Prospective patients in acute crisis are flown 1,400+ miles to Orange County at facility expense. Under California Health & Safety Code § 11831.6, providing free travel as an inducement is strictly illegal, yet routinely disguised as travel scholarships.

3. THE TRAP "Hostage Return Ticket"
Geographic Coercion to Day 30

The return flight home is strictly contingent on reaching the facility's designated graduation date. Leaving early forfeits paid transport. Penniless patients are coerced to stay so the facility can milk their insurance to the final authorized day.

The Municipal Fallout: This fly-in dynamic directly drives the coastal Orange County homeless crisis. When patients walk out early, leave AMA, or have their insurance authorizations denied, facilities revoke return transport, stranding out-of-state individuals 1,400 miles from home on the streets of Dana Point, San Clemente, and Costa Mesa.
